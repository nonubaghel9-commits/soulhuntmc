Join in three steps

Same address on both editions: play.soulhuntmc.xyz

Java Edition

  1. Open Minecraft and click Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server.
  3. Enter play.soulhuntmc.xyz as the address, save, and join.

Bedrock Edition

  1. Open Minecraft, then go to Play and Servers.
  2. Scroll down and click Add Server.
  3. Enter play.soulhuntmc.xyz as the address and 25565 as the port, save, and join.
