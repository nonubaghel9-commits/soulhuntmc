Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information SoulHuntMC (“we”, “us”) collects when you log in on www.soulhuntmc.xyz and how we use it.

What we collect

When you log in with Google, Discord or email, we receive only basic account details:

  • Your email address
  • Your name or username
  • Your profile picture (if the provider shares it)
  • A unique account ID

If you sign up with email and password, your password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.

How we use it

We use this information only to let you log in, show your name and picture on the website, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Login is handled by Supabase, and sign-in is provided by Google and Discord. These services process your data under their own privacy policies. The website is hosted on Cloudflare.

Cookies and storage

We store a login session in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. To have your account and data deleted, contact us through our Discord server and we will remove it.

Children

The website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

For any privacy question, reach us on our Discord server.
